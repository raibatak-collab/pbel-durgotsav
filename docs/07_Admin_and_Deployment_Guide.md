### 3. Admin Control Modules (10 Operational Modules)

1. **📊 Overview Dashboard:** Real-time verified seva funds, transaction count, volunteer count, and cultural performance count.
2. **💰 Contributions CRM & Anti-Fraud Verification:**
   - 1-click **Approve (✓)** (moves submission to `Success` and publishes on Wall of Honor).
   - 1-click **Reject (✕)** (discards invalid submissions).
   - **Edit Contributor**: Fix typos in resident name and flat number.
   - **Wall of Honor Visibility Toggle**: 1-click eye toggle to switch between Public and Anonymous ("Devout Well Wisher").
   - **Official Receipt**: Open printable certificate and click "Share WhatsApp" to message the donor directly.
   - **Copy Sponsors List**: Categorized copy tool for Maha Bhog, Sweets, and General Seva sponsors.
3. **🌺 Seva Offerings CMS:** Manage packages, set limits, and toggle active status.
4. **📅 Schedule (Pujo Nirghanto) CMS:** Real-time ritual and stage timing editor.
5. **🎥 Media & YouTube Video CMS:** Add, reorder, and manage YouTube videos and gallery photos.
6. **🤝 Volunteers Management:** View and export volunteer rosters across all 4 domains.
7. **🎭 Pratibimb Performers Management:** Review and approve township cultural acts.
8. **🏢 Corporate Sponsors CMS:** Add partner logos, assign tiers, and upload sponsorship decks.
9. **👥 Committee Wings CMS:** Manage organizing wings and member designations.
10. **🌐 Site Highlights & Announcement Pop-up CMS:** Configure pop-up alerts and path alpona announcements.
