# 3. UI/UX Design Specifications
## PBEL City Durgotsav 2026 Web Platform

### 1. Visual Identity & Brand Tokens

The design language embodies **Sacred Royal Elegance**:
- **Primary Sacred Crimson**: `#850E1F` / `#5E0A16` (Symbolizes Shakti, Maa Durga's sindoor, and regal festive warmth).
- **Secondary Devotional Gold**: `#D99B26` / `#B8801C` / `#FDE68A` (Radiates divine blessings, prosperity, and auspicious alpona).
- **Background Parchment**: `#FFFDF9` / `#FFF8EE` (Traditional temple-like sacred parchment tone).
- **Deep Slate Contrast**: `#1F0307` / `#111827` (Provides crystal-clear readability and contrast).

---

### 2. Devotee Wall of Honor Design (`/wall-of-honor`)

- **Sacred Hero Header**:
  - Bengali invocation: *॥ ভক্ত সম্মাননা ॥ • Devotee Wall of Honor*.
  - Real-time impact counters: Verified Devotees, Total Seva Raised (₹), Towers United, PSS Member Families.
- **Filter & Search Bar**:
  - Live search input matching Resident Name, Flat Number, or Seva offering.
  - HTML5 calendar date selector with "Clear Date" badge.
  - Horizontal scrollable Tower chips for all 14 PBEL towers and Well Wishers.
- **Devotee Card Architecture**:
  - Devotee initial avatar in gold gradient border.
  - Contributor Name (or "Devout Well Wisher").
  - PBEL Flat & Tower badge.
  - Exact Seva Offering Badge (e.g. *Maha Ashtami Flowers*, *Maha Ashtami Morning Puja*).
  - Auspicious Date of offering.
  - Interactive **"Memento Card 🪔"** action button.

---

### 3. Devotional Keepsake Memento Card Design

- **Traditional Framing**:
  - Double border with royal crimson (`#991B1B`) and antiqued gold (`#D4AF37`).
  - Auspicious corner motifs (`❖`).
  - Vector PSS Kalash Emblem and Maa Durga Trinetra icon.
- **Sacred Typography**:
  - Sanskrit Chant: *॥ সর্বমঙ্গল মঙ্গল্যে শিবে সর্বার্থ সাধিকে । শরণ্যে ত্র্যম্বকে গৌরি নারায়ণি নমোহস্তুতে ॥*
  - Commemorative Title: **"Thank You for Being Part of PBEL Durgotsav 2026"** (*ধন্যবাদ ও শারদীয় শুভকামনা*).
  - Devotee Recognition: Name, Flat, Tower, exact Seva Category, and contribution date.
  - **Zero Financial Amount**: Strictly spiritual recognition suitable for framing and proud sharing.
  - Official Verified Samiti Seal & President/General Secretary digitized signature.
  - Actions: Print / Save PDF and Share on WhatsApp.
